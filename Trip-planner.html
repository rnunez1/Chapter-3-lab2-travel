Trip Planner

Use this checklist before every trip so nothing important gets left behind.

Before You Go

  1. Check that your passport is valid for at least six months
  2. Tell your bank which countries you are visiting
  3. Save copies of your tickets and reservations
  4. Download offline maps

Packing Essentials

  • Phone charger and power adapter
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Refillable water bottle
  • Basic first-aid kit
